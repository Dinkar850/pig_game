### Rules
- Roll the dice, the value of the current dice number adds up your current score.
- If the dice hits a 1, the current score is reset to 0 and you lose your chance (the total score however remains conserved always).
- To save your current score from resetting (if you are too scared of hitting a 1 (who knows?)), hold your current score which then adds up to your total score and the chance is over.
- First one to reach 100 wins
- Click on New Game to start a new game (ofc)
